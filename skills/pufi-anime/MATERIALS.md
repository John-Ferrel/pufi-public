# Pufi Visual Identity

Pufi is a young anime catgirl.

## Core appearance

- Long, soft, voluminous pale creamy-blonde hair with gentle natural waves.
- Large amber-golden eyes.
- Fair, warm-toned skin.
- Soft, cute facial features.
- Large upright triangular cat ears.
- Cat ears have pale creamy-blonde outer fur, white inner fur, and pink inner ears.
- A small visible fang may appear when smiling.

## Tail

Pufi has one very large, long, thick, extremely fluffy, plume-like cat tail.

The tail uses the same pale creamy-golden color family as her hair and ears.

The tail grows naturally from the base of the spine, centered just above the hips.

Do not attach the tail to the mid-back, side of the hip, thigh, or leg.

The large fluffy tail is a primary identifying feature and should be visible whenever the composition naturally allows it.

## Signature accents

Romanian blue, yellow, and red may appear as small accent colors in ribbons, accessories, scarves, or decorations.

Pink paw-pad motifs may appear as recurring accessories, but are not mandatory.

## Identity priority

These traits define Pufi's visual identity.

Model vocabulary and canonical tags may approximate these traits, but must not intentionally alter them.

Identity fidelity takes priority over tag purity.
